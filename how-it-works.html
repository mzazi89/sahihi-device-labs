Method

A workflow you can audit at every step.

No remote access you cannot see, no promises that depend on luck. This is the sequence behind every MZAZI TECH engagement.

01 — Intake

You send the device model, IMEI where available, lock type and the service you need. We reply within one business day with the route and documents required.

Form → reference ID

02 — Ownership verification

Receipt, contract, provider statement and ID. If the device is financed, we confirm the account status with the provider before anything else.

Documents & authority

03 — Scoping

We write down the scope: what will be checked, what will be requested from the provider, turnaround and cost. You approve it before work starts.

Written scope, no surprises

04 — Service or release

Diagnostics and security work run in-house. Carrier and financing locks are released by the provider that applied them — we prepare and follow the request.

Provider-authorised

05 — Report handover

You receive the diagnostic sheet, lock-status summary, IMEI notes and, where applicable, written release confirmation from the provider.

PDF-ready pack

06 — Follow-up

One clarification round is included. If your case sits outside what we can lawfully do, we tell you which provider to approach and what to ask for.

Referral, not a sales pitch

Operating principles

Three rules that shape every job.

Consent before capability

If authority is unclear, we stop. A deadline never overrides verification, and a cash payment never shortcuts it.

Everything is observable

You see each step on screen or on the bench. There is no background process and no unexplained delay.

Claims match evidence

We report only what we observed. A device is not described as unlocked until the provider confirms it in writing.

Inside the report

What you actually receive.

A service record written for a non-technical reader, suitable for resale, warranty claims or insurance.

  • Cover sheet — reference, date, scope and technician.
  • Diagnostic sheet — battery, storage, thermal and patch-level fields with notes.
  • Lock-status summary — field-by-field explanation of each indicator.
  • IMEI structure page — TAC, serial segment and checksum result.
  • Provider correspondence — release request and confirmation, where applicable.

MZAZI TECH INC provides diagnostics, security testing and authorised lock-release support. We do not remove Factory Reset Protection, bypass account or payment locks, or unlock a device without verified proof of ownership and provider authorisation. Financing and carrier locks are released by the provider after settlement and verification; we coordinate that process and confirm the outcome in writing. Assessment results on this site are preliminary and subject to document verification.

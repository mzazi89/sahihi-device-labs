Boundaries

What we will not do — under any circumstances.

A legitimate diagnostics practice is defined as much by its refusals as by its capabilities. These requests are declined, every time.

No circumvention. We do not remove FRP, bypass activation or account locks, defeat payment locks, or unlock a device without documented proof of ownership and carrier authorisation.

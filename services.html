MZAZI TECH INC provides diagnostics, security testing and authorised lock-release support. We do not remove Factory Reset Protection, bypass account or payment locks, or unlock a device without verified proof of ownership and provider authorisation. Financing and carrier locks are released by the provider after settlement and verification; we coordinate that process and confirm the outcome in writing. Assessment results on this site are preliminary and subject to document verification.

Who we are

Built for clarity in a noisy industry.

MZAZI TECH INC models what a modern device-solutions practice should look like: documented, consent-driven and honest about the limits of what any technician can do.

A demonstration studio, not a repair shop.

The device-services market is full of promises that cannot be kept: “permanent unlock”, “FRP removed in minutes”, “payment lock bypass”. Those claims are usually illegal, frequently fraudulent, and always bad for the person holding the phone.

MZAZI TECH INC was created as the counter-example. We build interfaces and workflows that show how diagnostics really work, what a legitimate unlock actually requires, and where a responsible operator must stop.

“A device is not unlocked until the network says it is. Everything else is marketing.” MZAZI TECH operating note

What we value

Four principles, applied literally.

Clarity over jargon

Every report field is explained in plain language. If a client cannot understand the output, the output is not finished.

Consent before capability

Authority is verified before anything is demonstrated. Ownership questions are resolved in writing, not in conversation.

Evidence over claims

We report what we observed, label what we simulated, and never dress up a demo result as a real device outcome.

Refusal as a feature

A published list of things we decline is a quality signal. Ours includes FRP removal, account bypass and payment circumvention.

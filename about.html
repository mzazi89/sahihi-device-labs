Who we are

Built for clarity in a noisy industry.

SAHIHI DEVICE LABS LTD is a Nairobi device-solutions practice: diagnostics, security testing and authorised lock release, documented from intake to handover.

A service practice, not a shortcut shop.

The device-services market is full of promises that cannot be kept: “permanent unlock”, “FRP removed in minutes”, “payment lock bypass”. Those claims are usually illegal, frequently fraudulent, and always bad for the person holding the phone.

SAHIHI DEVICE LABS LTD was built as the counter-example. We diagnose what can be diagnosed, document what we find, and route lock release through the carrier or financier that owns the lock.

“A device is not unlocked until the network says it is. Everything else is marketing.”SAHIHI operating note

What we value

Four principles, applied literally.

Clarity over jargon

Every report field is explained in plain language. If a client cannot understand the output, the output is not finished.

Consent before capability

Authority is verified before any work is performed. Ownership questions are resolved with documents, not conversation.

Evidence over claims

We report what we observed and state what we could not verify. No result is dressed up to look better than it is.

Refusal as a feature

A published list of things we decline is a quality signal. Ours includes FRP removal, account bypass and active payment-lock circumvention.

SAHIHI DEVICE LABS LTD provides diagnostics, security testing and authorised lock-release support. We do not remove Factory Reset Protection, bypass account or payment locks, or unlock a device without verified proof of ownership and provider authorisation. Financing and carrier locks are released by the provider after settlement and verification; we coordinate that process and confirm the outcome in writing. Assessment results on this site are preliminary and subject to document verification.

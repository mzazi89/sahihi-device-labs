Our standard

A service practice built on evidence, not shortcuts.

Most “instant unlock” offers online are either fraudulent or illegal. We take the slower route: verify the device, confirm the owner, work through the provider, and document the result.

Ownership verified first

Receipt, contract or provider statement — checked before any work begins.

Release through the provider

Financing and carrier locks are cleared by the party that applied them, after settlement.

No hidden actions

No FRP removal, no account bypass, no remote access to your unlocked device.

SAHIHI DEVICE LABS LTD provides diagnostics, security testing and authorised lock-release support. We do not remove Factory Reset Protection, bypass account or payment locks, or unlock a device without verified proof of ownership and provider authorisation. Financing and carrier locks are released by the provider after settlement and verification; we coordinate that process and confirm the outcome in writing. Assessment results on this site are preliminary and subject to document verification.

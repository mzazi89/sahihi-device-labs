Our standard

A diagnostics practice built on evidence, not shortcuts.

MZAZI TECH INC operates as a demonstration studio. We model the parts of device servicing that are legitimate, documented and repeatable — and we are explicit about the parts that are not.

Every result is labelled

Simulated output carries a DEMO marker, a reference ID and a plain-language explanation of how it was produced.

No hidden actions

Nothing is sent to a device, a carrier or a database. The checker runs entirely in your browser.

Clear boundaries

We do not perform FRP removal, account bypasses, payment circumvention or unlocking without proof of ownership.
